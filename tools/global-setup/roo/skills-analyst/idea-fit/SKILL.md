# Idea fit

Use before CEO delegates work. The idea may be a digital solution or a media product. Method sources: coreyhaines31/marketingskills product profiles and AIDevGTM/gtm-cofounder pricing guides. Do not copy those repos.

1. Who pays: software buyers, subscribers, or sponsors. The job they need done.
2. Maximum three sources. Delegate to `browser` to open pages. Facts and assumptions written separately.
3. What they currently use instead, including manual workarounds. Competitor figures that have sources get the source and date. No source: write `no public source`. Do not estimate revenue, traffic, or customer counts.
4. Pricing lives in this document. No separate pricing agent.
   - Vietnam: one VND amount. International: one USD amount. Same tier or same unit.
   - Each price must anchor to what buyers currently pay for their existing approach. Do not use cost-to-build as the basis for pricing.
   - Software selling unit is one customer. Media selling unit is one episode, one sponsorship slot, or one monthly subscription.
   - Cost to deliver one unit. Conditions under which the unit is loss-making.
   - Free tier only when it is genuinely usable and leads to the paid tier.
5. One-line conclusion: build, build a small stoppable slice, or do not build. State the division: `giai-phap-so` or `media`.

Write `../shared/01_Projects/<domain>/<project-name>/idea-fit.md`. Domain is `giai-phap-so` or `media`. Two sections: `## Vietnamese` then `## English`. Do not write a spec. Do not write scripts.

Conclusion is do-not-build: stop. Do not call `architect` or `creator`. End with one next step.
