# Founder next moves

Use when a product or channel is live and the next step is needed. New ideas still go through `idea-fit` first. Do not use this skill to change prices in `idea-fit.md`.

Read `idea-fit.md` and `market-choice.md` if they exist. Missing a fact: write one assumption in the file and continue — do not stop to ask.

Write `../shared/01_Projects/<domain>/<project-name>/next-moves.md`. Include all sections:

- Current state: who is paying, where they are stuck, what has been tried.
- Three moves, heaviest first. Each move a different direction. Each move specific to this company. Do not use generic advice like "run ads" without naming the channel, audience, and metric.
- For each move: what to do, why it fits this company specifically, steps, measurement number, action doable in one hour. Numbers without sources are written as `no source` — do not estimate.
- Which move to start with and why.
- Which number after a period of time counts as success.

Any step that requires sending, posting, paying, or writing to production goes into an approval-needed step. Do not take that step. Do not write posts. That is `marketer` or `creator` work. Do not fabricate competitor figures.

Two sections `## Vietnamese` then `## English`. End with one next step.
