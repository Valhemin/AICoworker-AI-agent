# Market choice

Use to determine who to sell to, what they currently use, and which solution is worth building.

1. Write the user, the job they need done, and known constraints.
2. Maximum three sources. Delegate to `browser` to open pages. Keep facts and inferences separate. Numbers without sources are written as `no public source` — do not estimate.
3. List two or three options. For each: who uses it, cost, risk, reason to drop. If a known weakness appears in a source, write that weakness and one thing this product can do because of it.
4. Commit to one option. If sources are weak, say so and choose the smallest reversible step. Predictions about what a competitor will do are labeled as inference, with the observed signal noted.
5. Write `../shared/01_Projects/<domain>/<project-name>/market-choice.md`. Do not write a content campaign. That is `creator` work.

End the report with one next step.
