# Deal brief

Use immediately after every meeting or call with a prospect. Write while memory is fresh.

Sources: AIDevGTM founder-led-sales, TheCraigHewitt sales-skills. Do not copy code.

Read `../shared/03_Brand/<project-name>/sales-context.md` for reference.

## Input

User recounts the meeting. Agent helps fill in the template.

## Write `../shared/03_Brand/<project-name>/deals/<name>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`

```
## Deal: <Name / company>
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Four factors
- **Champion:** <Who will use it and wants to buy? Still blank?>
- **Buyer:** <Who controls the budget? Still blank?>
- **Pain:** <What is the specific problem? What does it cost them?>
- **Trigger:** <Why must it be solved now? Still blank?>

## Agreed next step
- Action: <specific>
- Deadline: <specific date>
- Owner: <me or them>

## Risk signals
- [ ] Have not spoken to the budget holder yet
- [ ] No clear trigger
- [ ] They are evaluating [competitor]
- [ ] Could not set a next step with a date

## I need to send the champion
<If they need to "arm the champion" to convince internally: a short business case>
- Problem: ...
- How the product solves it: ...
- Why now: ...
- Price: ...
```

## After writing

- A factor is still blank → suggest questions to ask next time.
- No dated next step → remind: no next step = no deal.
- Risk signal present → suggest a specific `objection-handling` response.
- All four factors complete + next step exists → suggest `deal-close` when the decision date approaches.
