# Product brand

Use in the same session as `DESIGN.md` when the product will be sold. Skip for `developer-work` tasks.

1. One mark: what it looks like, which color from `DESIGN.md`, where it must not be placed.
2. One tagline. `ceo` gate: a Vietnamese sentence and an English sentence, same meaning.
3. Usage rules for ad images, videos, and guides. Do not introduce a second visual direction.

Write `docs/design/BRAND.md`. `ceo` gate: two sections `## Vietnamese` and `## English`. Do not export image files. `creator-visual` produces images from this document. End with one next step.
