# Vietnam market

Apply when users are in Vietnam or the user did not name another market. Cite the article in the checklist row you output. Do not paste the statute into the product.

## Personal data — always if the product processes it

Law 91/2025/QH15 on personal data protection, in force 2026-01-01. Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP guides it and replaces Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP. Decree 13 is not the current instrument.

The product must:

- Tell the person, before processing, the data types, the purpose, who controls the data, and their rights. Consent is separate per purpose, recordable, and not inferred from silence or from a bundled checkbox (Điều 9).
- Provide a way to view, correct, receive a copy, delete, restrict processing, and withdraw consent (Điều 4, 10, 13, 14, 15).
- Keep data only for the stated purpose. Document the retention period in the notice (Điều 3).
- If the data is sensitive under Decree 356/2025 Điều 4, say so in the consent. Sensitive categories include race or ethnicity, political or religious views, private and family life, health, biometrics and genetics, sex life, precise location, images of identity cards, electronic-identity or bank logins and payment history, and behavioral tracking listed in that article. Read Điều 4 before narrowing the list.
- Children: a parent or guardian exercises the child's rights. Publishing a child's private life from age 7 needs both the child and the guardian (Điều 24).
- Ads and tracking pixels: consent before tracking, and a way to refuse further marketing (Điều 28).
- Serious incidents (national security, life, health, dignity, or property): notify the police data-protection unit within 72 hours of discovery (Điều 23). Ship the runbook path (who is told, what is recorded). Do not build the government form inside the app.
- Cross-border transfer (storage outside Vietnam, or a foreign platform processing data collected in Vietnam): Điều 20 requires an impact file sent within 60 days of the first transfer, with the exceptions in khoản 6. Using a foreign host, auth provider, or analytics tool usually is a transfer. Flag it. The small-business deferral below does not cover Điều 20.
- Impact assessment of processing (Điều 21) and appointing a data-protection person (khoản 2 Điều 33) are required unless an exemption applies.

Exemption, Luật 91/2025 Điều 38 and Decree 356/2025 Điều 41–42:

- Small enterprises and startups may choose to skip Điều 21, Điều 22, and khoản 2 Điều 33 for five years from 2026-01-01.
- Micro enterprises and household businesses are not required to do those three items.
- The exemption is lost if they sell personal-data processing services, directly process sensitive personal data, or have processed data of 100,000 or more people.
- Consent, rights, notice, ads, children, and breach notice are not exempt.

Do not buy or sell personal data (Điều 7).

## Consumer sale — website or desktop

Law 19/2023/QH15 on consumer protection, in force 2024-07-01. A remote sale includes an app (Điều 37).

Before the user agrees, show: legal name, address, phone, business registration or personal tax code, product characteristics, quantity, origin, price, delivery cost, what is included in the price (VAT and other fees), payment method and timing, delivery window, return and cancellation steps, warranty, how long the offer lasts, and how to complain.

The contract text is readable before acceptance. The user can review it and download the accepted copy (Điều 38).

If the business collects consumer information, the public rule states purpose, scope, retention, and safeguards, on the site and in the app, before collection (Điều 16).

## E-commerce — own store or marketplace

Decree 52/2013/NĐ-CP as amended by Decree 85/2021/NĐ-CP. "Website" in this decree includes the app the business uses to sell.

Own store (sells the owner's goods or services): notify Bộ Công Thương. Marketplace, online promotion platform, or online auction: register. Both are OWNER ACTION on online.gov.vn. Do not paint the ministry logo until the user gives the listing URL.

On the home screen, show owner name, head-office or residential address, business registration or tax code, and a phone or other direct contact (Điều 29).

Product information must be enough to identify the goods, including mandatory label facts except lot-level facts such as manufacture date and batch (Điều 30 as amended).

Price must say whether tax, packing, and shipping are included (Điều 31).

General terms, in Vietnamese and in contrasting type: limits, inspection policy, return window, method, refund path, return cost, warranty, service standards, and each side's duties (Điều 32 as amended). Online checkout needs a separate acceptance of those terms.

Shipping methods, estimated time, geographic limits, and a way to cancel if delivery is late (Điều 33).

Every payment method explained. Online payment shows the transaction and requires confirmation before charge (Điều 34).

After an online order is accepted, show the line items, prices, total, delivery time, and a contact for order status (Điều 19).

Continuous services (subscriptions and ongoing online services): a control to send termination, keep a copy, and show that it was received (Điều 22).

A marketplace also needs seller identity, a complaint path between buyer and seller, and notice to users at least five days before material term changes.

## Internet publication

Decree 147/2024/NĐ-CP, in force 2024-12-25. An application is within the definition of an electronic information page.

- The operator is responsible for content it stores or transmits.
- Do not disclose user information except with consent or a lawful authority request.
- A site that aggregates news, operates as a social network, or publishes as a newspaper needs the license that decree and press law require. Stop and list it. Do not treat it as an ordinary website.
- Prohibited content stays out. User-generated content needs a report control and a takedown path.
- An international domain used by a Vietnamese organization is notified to the ministry responsible for internet resources. OWNER ACTION.

Law 24/2018/QH14 on cybersecurity and Decree 53/2022/NĐ-CP: storing data in Vietnam and opening a local entity are not the default for every app. They follow a written request from the Ministry of Public Security after the service fails to comply with a lawful takedown or cooperation request. Record the trigger. Do not build a Vietnam-only data center unless the user asks or that order is in the task.

## Electronic contracts and tax

Law 20/2023/QH15 on electronic transactions: the contract and the acceptance must remain retrievable and intact. A click that cannot be saved does not meet Điều 38 of the consumer law.

Businesses that sell issue e-invoices under the current tax rules (Decree 123/2020/NĐ-CP as amended, including Decree 70/2025/NĐ-CP). Integrating an invoice provider is in scope only when the user asked for invoicing. Otherwise list e-invoice registration as OWNER ACTION.

## Not required on every site

A personal or company brochure with no account, no personal-data form, and no sale does not need a checkout, return policy, or ministry filing. It still needs an owner contact if it asks for personal data, and the privacy notice if it processes any.
