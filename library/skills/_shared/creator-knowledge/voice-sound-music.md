# Voice, Sound, and Music Playbook

## Audio hierarchy

Decide the listening priority by moment:

1. intelligible speech or essential sound;
2. narrative/action cues;
3. ambience and world;
4. music and emotional framing;
5. decorative effects.

Loudness alone cannot solve competing frequencies or dense arrangement.

## Voice direction

```yaml
  language_and_locale:

  pronunciation_guide: []
  breaths_and_pauses:
  forbidden_imitation:
  consent_and_rights:
```

Use performance verbs and situation. Avoid requesting an exact living person’s voice without authorization.

## Scratch versus final

Create scratch voice early for timing. Lock script, pronunciation, and edit rhythm before final synthesis or recording. Maintain alternate takes for emotional turns.

## Sound design functions

- orient space and time;
- reveal off-screen action;
- motivate a cut;
- create anticipation;
- establish motif;
- clarify interface or process;
- support impact and physical weight;
- transition between scenes.

Do not add sound effects to every visual change.

## Music brief

Define narrative job, arc, tempo range, instrumentation, density, cultural context, entry/exit points, and dialogue space. A reference track does not grant style-copying or sync rights.

## Localization

Speech timing, pronunciation, and music meaning can change by market. Keep stems, allow timing changes, and review culturally specific instruments or vocal styles.

## Rights and provenance

Record performer consent, voice-model permission, music license, generated-music terms, sample rights, and platform restrictions. “Royalty-free” does not always mean unrestricted or free.

## QC

- speech intelligibility on phone speakers;
- no clipping or severe noise;
- consistent room tone;
- subtitle/voice synchronization;
- correct language and pronunciation;
- loudness appropriate to delivery platform;
- mono compatibility where relevant;
- no accidental copyrighted source audio.

## Acceptance criteria

The artifact is decision-ready, exposes assumptions and unresolved risk, follows the named target market and routine, and gives downstream owners enough structured information to proceed without inventing critical details.
