# Content Strategy Playbook

## Strategic chain

`business or creative outcome → audience change → content job → format → distribution → measurement → learning`

A content pillar is not a list of topics. It is a repeatable promise and mechanism for a defined audience need.

## Funnel roles

- **Discovery**: create recognition, curiosity, or emotional identification.
- **Consideration**: clarify a problem, mechanism, method, or differentiation.
- **Proof**: demonstrate outcomes, evidence, process, expertise, or social validation.
- **Conversion**: reduce objections and make the next action low-friction.
- **Retention/community**: deepen identity, habit, participation, or progress.

One asset can support more than one role, but it needs one primary job.

## Format selection

Choose format by decision need, not habit:

- demonstration when the mechanism is visual;
- testimonial when trust transfer matters and consent is clear;
- narrative when change, identity, or stakes matter;
- explainer when a misconception blocks progress;
- comparison when the audience is choosing between options;
- live or interview when uncertainty and interaction create value;
- carousel/article when scanning, reference, or search matters;
- series when the concept has repeatable tension and memory.

## Content system design

For each repeatable format define:

```yaml
format_system:
  audience_job:
  promise:
  repeatable_mechanism:
  opening_patterns: []
  proof_patterns: []
  visual_grammar:
  host_or_character_role:
  typical_duration:
  production_tier:
  reusable_assets: []
  variation_space: []
  fatigue_signals: []
  success_metrics: []
```

A sustainable series has a stable container and variable substance.

## Portfolio balance

Use a portfolio rather than asking every post to do everything:

- 50–70% proven formats and themes;
- 20–30% adjacent experiments;
- 10–20% high-variance exploration.

Adjust the split to risk tolerance, publishing volume, and stage of the channel.

## Platform adaptation

Do not simply crop one master. Decide what must change:

- opening frame and context;
- duration and information density;
- caption style and safe zones;
- title/thumbnail or cover behavior;
- CTA and link path;
- audio assumptions;
- audience awareness level;
- platform-native interaction.

## Measurement tree

Tie metrics to the content job:

- reach alone is not proof of qualified interest;
- retention without action may indicate entertainment but weak relevance;
- clicks without downstream quality may indicate misleading packaging;
- saves may indicate utility but not immediate conversion;
- comprehension requires assessment, not views;
- brand lift and long-cycle B2B impact may need surveys or assisted-conversion analysis.

Define one primary metric, diagnostic metrics, and guardrails.

## Failure patterns

- topic calendar with no audience job;
- platform frequency treated as strategy;
- every asset ends with the same CTA;
- success judged by vanity metrics;
- repurposing without changing platform function;
- creator voice inconsistent across formats;
- experiments too similar to learn anything.

## Cost controls

Create high-cost hero assets only when they generate reusable components or support high-value distribution. Use a modular production plan: master research, scripts, shoots, visual assets, and proof can feed multiple outputs. Do not multiply low-value derivatives simply because automation makes them cheap.
