# Children and Family Content Playbook

## Purpose

Create age-appropriate, emotionally safe, comprehensible content for children and families without treating children as a single audience. Developmental stage, caregiver context, target market, language, and platform determine the design.

## Audience bands

Use bands as planning prompts, not diagnoses:

- early childhood: concrete language, repetition, strong visual causality, limited working-memory demand;
- early primary: simple rules, visible goals, playful prediction, basic perspective taking;
- upper primary: richer cause/effect, social tension, humor, competence and discovery;
- early teens: identity, belonging, autonomy, status, fairness, and more complex irony;
- family co-viewing: layered meaning that remains safe and understandable for younger viewers.

Validate age classifications and platform rules in the target market.

## Design principles

1. State or demonstrate the central activity quickly.
2. Use concrete actions before abstract explanation.
3. Repeat with variation rather than exact redundancy.
4. Make emotional changes legible through behavior and visual state.
5. Give child characters agency and meaningful choices.
6. Avoid humiliation, manipulative fear, unsafe imitation, and adult-coded subtext.
7. Keep commercial intent and sponsorship clear to caregivers and age-appropriate audiences.

## Learning and comprehension

For instructional content:

```yaml
child_learning_unit:
  observable_objective:
  prior_knowledge:
  likely_misconception:
  demonstration:
  guided_try:
  independent_check:
  caregiver_extension:
```

Use retrieval or a simple action check instead of asking “Do you understand?” Visual excitement must not obscure the target concept.

## Story design

- define a visible want and understandable obstacle;
- keep consequences proportionate and emotionally recoverable;
- let problem solving emerge from choices, cooperation, observation, or learned skills;
- show repair after conflict;
- avoid moral lectures after the lesson is already demonstrated;
- use recurring structures and characters to support familiarity without formulaic repetition.

## Dialogue and language

- write for speakability and actual age, not simplified adult corporate language;
- prefer short clauses but preserve natural rhythm;
- explain uncommon terms through context and action;
- localize names, school/family assumptions, humor, politeness, and caregiver roles with market review;
- avoid baby talk unless a specific character and context require it.

## Visual and audio safety

- maintain readable contrast and uncrowded frames;
- avoid excessive flashing, startling volume, and relentless rapid cuts;
- make captions, narration, and visual actions mutually supportive;
- design clear foreground/background separation;
- test whether important text remains visible behind platform UI.

## Representation

Do not use a token checklist. Give characters competence, flaws, relationships, and agency. Review stereotypes related to family structures, disability, gender, ethnicity, class, religion, accents, and geography in the target market.

## AI production controls

- obtain valid consent for real children’s likeness or voice;
- do not create deceptive synthetic depictions of real minors;
- lock character age, proportions, wardrobe, and behavior in the bible;
- inspect hands, facial expression, body position, unsafe objects, and background details;
- keep provenance and moderation records for generated assets.

## Failure patterns

- stimulation replaces meaning;
- adults solve every problem;
- danger is imitable without context;
- the “lesson” is delivered as a speech;
- humor depends on cruelty or embarrassment;
- localization changes only words while school/family context remains foreign;
- product persuasion is disguised as neutral education.

## Acceptance criteria

A local reviewer or qualified owner confirms age and cultural fit when risk warrants it; the child audience can state or demonstrate the intended takeaway; no critical safety, consent, rights, or deceptive-commercial gate remains unresolved.
