# Brand profile

Use for the first time when a product has been concluded as build by CEO and no brand profile exists yet. Use again when price or segment changes.

Read in order:
1. `../shared/01_Projects/<domain>/<project-name>/idea-fit.md` — price, who pays, current alternative.
2. `../shared/01_Projects/<domain>/<project-name>/market-choice.md` — chosen segment.
3. `docs/design/BRAND.md` in the product repo if designer has done it — colors, fonts, voice.

If any file is missing, state clearly which file is absent and continue with the available information. Do not stop.

## Write to `../shared/03_Brand/<project-name>/profile.md`

**One-sentence positioning:** For [who], [product] achieves [result], unlike [current alternative] because [real differentiator].

**Voice:** Three words describing the voice. Example: "direct, friendly, no pretending to be an expert". This voice applies to all captions, headlines, and invite copy.

**First customer group:** Group name, the job they currently do manually or expensively, what they are afraid of losing.

**Defensible differentiators:** Maximum three. Each has evidence from idea-fit or can be observed. Do not write a differentiator without evidence.

**Nearest competitor:** Name, their price if known, their weakness. Source from idea-fit.md or browser if needed. Maximum two competitors.

**Current price:** Taken verbatim from idea-fit.md. Do not change.

---

Do not fabricate evidence. Do not use words like "number one", "best", or "unique" without a source. Do not write future plans.

After writing: suggest the next step as `audience-channel` or `competitor-watch`.
