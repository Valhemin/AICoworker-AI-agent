# Customer guide

One path for the customer to complete the primary job. Do not write internal documentation.

1. The job the customer needs done, in five steps or fewer.
2. Each step: what they see, what they click, what they have when done.
3. One common error and how to recover.
4. `ceo` gate: write `## Vietnamese` then `## English`, same steps. Other gates: Vietnamese only.

Write `docs/launch/customer-guide.md` in the project. Do not post. End with one next step.
